Dataset Statistics

Code2Video-Compass combines 385 independent tasks with 1,719 aligned tasks derived from 573 source videos, covering T2V, I2V, and V2V.

Leaderboard

Results for 15 MLLMs, grouped into proprietary and open-weight models. The Overall tab reports pooled scores over all tasks across T2V, I2V, and V2V, as in the paper. Hover a column header for the metric definition.

Model results across T2V, I2V, and V2V.

Avg. Rank averages per-metric ranks across all 15 models, excluding Operational metrics (ES and TC); ties receive average ranks, and models are ordered by Avg. Rank within each group. The 12 objective metrics use the purple scale and the 5 subjective dimensions the green scale — darker is better. Column arrows mark each metric's direction (↑ higher is better, ↓ lower is better). Bold marks the best and underline the second-best score within each model group. ES is the delivered-video rate; TC the mean output tokens over tasks with recorded usage. A dash indicates a pending result.
